How to Test Buttons

Buttons are interactive elements that trigger actions when clicked. Test the following:

- Click behavior: Verify the button performs its intended action (submit, cancel, navigate, etc.)
- Disabled state: When disabled, the button should not respond to clicks
- Loading state: If the action is async, the button may show a spinner or "Loading..." and be disabled during the request
- Accessibility: Buttons should be focusable via keyboard (Tab) and activatable with Enter/Space
- Visual feedback: Hover and active (pressed) states should be visible
- Multiple rapid clicks: Ensure the action is not triggered multiple times (debouncing or disabling after first click)
- Button text and icons: Confirm labels are correct and icons render properly
